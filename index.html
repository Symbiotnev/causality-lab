Causality Labs

The verified record behind every small business, and the valuation lenders and insurers can trust.

Business plan and partner pitch. Kenya. Prepared by Nevill Muiruri Mwangi, Founder.

1. The idea in brief

Causality Labs is a results-driven business operating system for small and medium enterprises (SMEs). Businesses use it every day to record stock, sales, quotes, invoices, suppliers, materials and costs. In return it shows them why they make or lose money and how to improve it.

Because the records are verified against bank and M-Pesa statements, they also become the most accurate picture of the business that exists. That picture lets banks and insurers finance and insure businesses they cannot see clearly today. Our goal is to become the most trusted valuer in the SME space, starting with businesses that buy and sell or produce goods, then expanding to every kind of business.

Our position: we do not lend, broker or disburse. We supply verified operating data and valuations. Banks and insurers make their own decisions, and they are our partners.

2. The problem

  • Owners cannot see why profit moves. They know cash comes in and goes out, but not which products, prices, suppliers or expenses make the money or lose it.
  • Generic systems do not get used. They record data but do not give results, and they are not shaped around how the owner works, so owners drop them.
  • Lenders and insurers price SMEs blind. They rely on credit bureau listings, bank statements and M-Pesa statements. These show money moving but not stock, margins, costs or behaviour, so good businesses are declined or overcharged, especially for unsecured loans.

3. The solution

Causality is built in layers. Each layer depends on the one below it, and each makes the whole system harder to copy.

LayerWhat it does
1. CapturePoint of sale, inventory, quotes, invoices, suppliers, materials and production costs, expenses, multiple stores and staff. Simple enough to use daily.
2. InsightCost of goods against sales, price variance, profit margins, expense patterns, and which activities make or lose money, with clear recommendations.
3. VerificationRecorded transactions are matched against bank and M-Pesa statements. Every report carries a verification level, for example the share of recorded sales matched to real money received.
4. ProfileOwner goals and aspirations, the reason for any financing need, and, with explicit consent, repayment behaviour and group-savings signals.
5. ValuationStock valued by its potential, repayment capacity, and insurable risk, updated continuously as the business keeps recording.
6. NetworkBusinesses and suppliers connect on one platform, trade with each other, and build a shared, verified trading history.

Built with the owner, not for a crowd

Each business starts with what it needs most. We build the daily tools around that, then add results. Owners who help shape their system log in daily and tie their business to it. Under the surface is one common data model with ready-made templates for retail, manufacturing and services, so setup stays fast and the data stays comparable.

Verification without touching the money

Payment gateway fees and large-scale mobile money integrations are barriers for small businesses. So the business keeps taking payments the way it does today. We import bank and M-Pesa statements, match them to recorded sales, and flag the gaps. A gateway stays an optional upgrade. Statements are taken directly from the issuer wherever possible, with checks to detect edited files.

4. Why it grows in value

  1. A useful tool brings daily recording.
  2. Daily recording builds rich, verified data.
  3. Verified data produces accurate valuations.
  4. Accurate valuations unlock better financing and insurance for the business.
  5. A growing business records more, and the cycle repeats.

As each client grows, the value of the portfolios we present to banks and insurers grows with it. We earn more when clients succeed, which keeps our interests aligned with theirs.

9. Legal and regulatory requirements

We treat compliance as part of the product. These are the areas we will address from the start. Each must be confirmed with a qualified Kenyan lawyer before launch.

AreaRequirement and our approach
Company and taxRegister the company with the Business Registration Service, obtain a KRA PIN, and keep proper accounts and filings.
Data protectionData Protection Act, 2019: register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as a data controller or processor, collect specific and revocable consent for each use and each sharing purpose, limit data to what is needed, secure it, and honour access and deletion rights.
Credit informationBanking (Credit Reference Bureau) Regulations, 2020: credit reference bureaus need a Central Bank licence. We will be a data and analytics provider under contract to each partner, not a bureau, and will obtain a written legal opinion on this positioning.
LendingWe do not lend, broker or disburse. Banks issue approvals and loans. Our screens say “eligible for review by [Bank]” and never present an approval as ours.
PaymentsNational Payment System Act, 2011: we do not process or hold client funds. Payment gateways stay optional. Any network feature that moves money will be checked against payment service provider rules before launch.
InsuranceInsurance Regulatory Authority rules apply if we refer clients or sell cover. We will supply risk data to insurers and take the right registration if we refer.
Cyber and securityComputer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018, plus an independent security audit, encryption of data, access controls and audit logs.
Financial assuranceA CPA firm attests to our methodology and sampling. We describe ourselves as registered, audited and compliant, never as licensed unless a licence is actually held.
ContractsClear client terms covering consent and data use, a data-sharing agreement with each bank or insurer, and agreements that keep the company’s intellectual property clean.

10. Key risks and how we manage them

  • Slow client growth. We lead with results for the owner, build with each client, and recruit through partners.
  • Data accuracy. Statement matching, verification levels, independent review and tamper checks.
  • Long wait for bank revenue. A monthly runway plan, SME subscriptions, pilot fees and funding.
  • Small pilot samples. Leading indicators, honest limits on claims, and a second region to follow.
  • Regulatory interpretation. Early legal opinions and a data-only position.
